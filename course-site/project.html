    PROJECT ORGANISATION

    Team, assessment and submissions

    100 marks

    Form your engineering team

    • 4–6 students; choose a team/company name.
    • Nominate a coordinator. Share design, modelling, simulation, programming and communication responsibilities.
    • Every member must understand the complete system.
    • No more than three teams per robot manufacturer.

    Final submission

    1. Team-written report: design, modelling, validation and integrated results. Include a contribution statement and AI Use Declaration.
    2. Runnable files: upload MATLAB/Python scripts and relevant simulation/program files before presenting.
    3. Presentation and demonstration: approximately 15 minutes, then 3–5 minutes of questions. Three to six members may present; all members must be ready to answer.

    Detailed marking criteria

    Report · 35 marks

    • System concept, robot/end-effector and workcell (5)
    • Pose representation, kinematics and validation (8)
    • Trajectory, Jacobian, programming and integration (10)
    • Engineering justification and report quality (7)
    • Selected advanced challenge (5)

    Presentation & demonstration · 40 marks

    • Structure and audience-focused narrative (10)
    • Delivery, visuals and time management (10)
    • Explanation of the integrated demonstration (10), including 5 for communicating the advanced challenge
    • Q&A and coordinated team response (10)
